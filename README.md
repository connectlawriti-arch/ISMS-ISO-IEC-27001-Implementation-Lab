Project Overview

This repository is a practical learning and implementation lab focused on establishing, implementing, maintaining, auditing, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS) in alignment with ISO/IEC 27001.

The project translates ISMS requirements into practical activities, documentation, risk management processes, information security controls, audit procedures, and continual improvement measures.

It is intended to demonstrate the practical application of information security governance, risk-based decision-making, control implementation, and compliance assessment through structured documentation and illustrative case studies.

2. Project Objectives

The key objectives of this project are to:

Understand the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001 and the guidance provided by ISO/IEC 27002.
Develop a structured approach to ISMS establishment and implementation.
Identify information assets, assess information security risks, and determine appropriate risk treatment measures.
Understand the selection, implementation, and evaluation of information security controls.
Develop practical ISMS documentation, registers, checklists, and implementation trackers.
Establish an audit-oriented approach to evidence collection, nonconformity identification, corrective action, and follow-up.
Demonstrate continual improvement through monitoring, measurement, review, and risk reassessment.
3. ISMS Scope

For the purposes of this lab, the scope covers the governance and operational processes required to manage information security within a defined organisational environment.

The illustrative scope includes:

Information assets, including information, applications, infrastructure, endpoints, and supporting technology.
Information security governance, policies, roles, responsibilities, and accountability.
Identity and access management.
Asset management and information classification.
Risk assessment and risk treatment.
Security operations, incident management, and vulnerability management.
Supplier and third-party information security.
Business continuity and information security awareness.
Internal audit, management review, corrective action, and continual improvement.

Scope note: This is an illustrative learning scope, not a formally approved organisational ISMS scope. In an actual implementation, the scope must be defined and justified based on organisational context, interested parties, applicable requirements, interfaces, dependencies, and business activities.
